01 Pitch & Technical sheet

Awaken as a spirit with no memory, lost within ancient ruins. Take up a spiritual sword — your only companion, capable of cutting through matter and pulling your soul towards it.

Cross forgotten temples, avoid deadly traps and face the spirits of ancient guardians to recover the fragments of your past.

In Spirit Dash, every sword throw is a choice: strike, flee, or dash through the void.

Genre Action / Puzzle Platformer / RPG
Audience 13+ — Achievers, Thinkers, players who enjoy precision

02 Core mechanic — The spiritual sword

The spiritual sword is at the heart of the entire experience. It is not just a weapon: it is a mobility tool, a puzzle-solver, an extension of the player's soul.

⚔
Melee

Direct attack against enemies haunting the ruins. Fast, precise, risky — it demands close range.

◈
Throw & Teleport

Throw the blade at a surface or enemy and instantly dash to its location. Cross chasms, dodge traps, chain movements together.

⬡
Surfaces & Constraints

Some surfaces absorb or repel the blade, forcing the player to anticipate their trajectory. Every level becomes a mobility puzzle.

✦
03 Game loop — Fail, learn, transcend
I
Observation

Scout compatible surfaces, traps, enemies and possible trajectories. Every level is read before it is played.

II
Action

Throw the sword, teleport, dodge, fight. Tension and fluidity are the keys to gameplay pleasure. Aim, throw, teleport, strike, move on — no pause.

III
Failure or success

Contact with a trap or loss of energy → instant restart. Exit reached → memory fragment unlocked. Every death is fast, every restart is a lesson.

IV
Progression

Each fragment reveals part of the protagonist's story and strengthens the bond with the sword — minor improvements to range, speed or precision.
